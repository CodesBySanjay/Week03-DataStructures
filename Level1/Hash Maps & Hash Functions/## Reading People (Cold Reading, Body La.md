### Example: Anchoring (NLP)

1. Get someone to recall a **positive memory** (e.g., “Think of a time you felt powerful/confident.”)
2. As they describe it and feel it, **tap their shoulder** gently and consistently.
3. Repeat a few times (with same emotion + same touch).
4. Later, tap the same spot — they may **recall the emotion** involuntarily. That’s anchoring.

### Example: Cold Reading

Say to someone:
> “You’re the kind of person who’s usually calm, but when things build up, it hits hard. You don’t always show it, but you feel things deeply.”

→ Most people relate to this, so it feels personal. Combine that with good observation (e.g., tired eyes, clenched jaw) and make educated guesses.
